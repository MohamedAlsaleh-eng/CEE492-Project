Figure 1 - Distribution of compressive strength by specimen age (raincloud plot)
File: fig1_raincloud_age.png

WHAT IS PLOTTED
- x-axis: the 14 test ages in the dataset (days). The ages are shown as categories,
  evenly spaced, not on a time scale.
- y-axis: compressive strength (MPa).
- Numbers along the top: number of specimens (n) tested at each age.

HOW TO READ EACH AGE GROUP
Each age has three parts, from left to right:
1. Cloud (half violin): a kernel density estimate of the strength values. Wider means
   more specimens near that strength. Drawn only when n >= 10.
2. Box: the box spans Q1 to Q3 (the middle 50% of specimens), the thick line is the
   median, and the whiskers reach the most extreme values within 1.5 x IQR. Drawn
   when n >= 5.
3. Dots: one dot per specimen. They are spread sideways at random only so they do not
   overlap; horizontal position inside a group has no meaning.
