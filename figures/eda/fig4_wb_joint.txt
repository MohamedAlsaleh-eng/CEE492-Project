Figure 4 - Compressive strength versus water-to-binder ratio by specimen age (joint plot)
File: fig4_wb_joint.png

WHAT IS PLOTTED
- x-axis: water-to-binder ratio, w/b = water / (cement + slag + fly ash). This is a
  derived variable computed from the dataset columns.
- y-axis: compressive strength (MPa).
- One dot per specimen, all 1,030 specimens.
- Dot colour = specimen age on a log colour scale (purple = early, about 1-7 days;
  orange to yellow = late, 90-365 days). See the colour bar at top right.
- Grey histogram on top: distribution of w/b.
  Grey histogram on the right: distribution of strength.
