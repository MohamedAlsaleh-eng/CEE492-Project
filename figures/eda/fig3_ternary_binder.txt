Figure 3 - Binder composition and 28-day compressive strength (ternary diagram)
File: fig3_ternary_binder.png

WHAT IS PLOTTED
- Only the 425 specimens tested at 28 days, so that age does not affect the comparison.
- Each dot is one specimen. Its position gives the composition of its binder
  (cement + slag + fly ash) as mass shares that add up to 100%.
- Dot colour = 28-day compressive strength (colour bar on the right; yellow = high,
  purple = low).

HOW TO READ A TERNARY DIAGRAM
- The three corners are 100% cement (bottom left), 100% fly ash (bottom right) and
  100% slag (top).
- The closer a dot is to a corner, the larger the share of that material.
- Dots on the bottom edge contain no slag (cement + fly ash only).
  Dots on the left edge contain no fly ash (cement + slag only).
  Dots exactly at the bottom-left corner are Portland cement only.
  Dots inside the triangle contain all three materials.
- To read a share, follow the grid lines to the tick labels:
  bottom edge = fly ash %, right edge = slag %, left edge = cement %.
