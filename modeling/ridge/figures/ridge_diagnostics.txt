Figure - Test-set diagnostics of polynomial regression with ridge regularization
File: ridge_diagnostics.png (vector version ridge_diagnostics.pdf is produced locally; *.pdf may be git-ignored)
Code: modeling/ridge/ridge.py

All three panels show the 201 held-out test specimens. The test mixtures were
not included in training. Blue dots show individual predictions. Residual =
observed - predicted strength, so a NEGATIVE residual means that the model
OVERPREDICTED strength, and a POSITIVE residual means it UNDERPREDICTED.

PANEL (a) - predicted vs observed strength
- x-axis: measured concrete compressive strength (MPa).
- y-axis: model-predicted compressive strength (MPa).
- Grey diagonal: perfect predictions, where predicted = observed.
- Light grey band: predictions within +/-10 MPa of the observed value.
- The text gives the test sample size (201) and test RMSE (7.62 MPa).

What to look for: many points are close to the diagonal, but errors remain,
particularly at higher strengths. The polynomial ridge model performs better
than the original eight-variable MLR (10.39 MPa RMSE), but not as well as the
selected MLR with ln(age) and water-to-binder ratio (6.77 MPa RMSE).

PANEL (b) - residual vs specimen age
- x-axis: specimen age in days on a logarithmic scale
- y-axis: residual, observed minus predicted, in MPa
- Horizontal grey line: zero residual (no prediction error)
- Black line with square markers: median residual for the plotted age groups.

What to look for: errors vary with age. The median residual is negative for
some younger specimens, becomes positive around intermediate ages, and turns
negative again for older specimens. Among the 14 test specimens aged 180 days
or more, RMSE is 10.63 MPa and the average residual is -2.93 MPa, meaning the
model tends to overpredict these older specimens.

PANEL (c) - residual vs predicted strength
- x-axis: predicted strength (MPa).
- y-axis: residual, observed minus predicted, in MPa.
- Black curve: LOWESS smoothed trend of the residuals.
- Dashed horizontal lines: residuals of +10 and -10 MPa (not confidence limits).

What to look for: the LOWESS trend is not flat at zero, suggesting some
remaining systematic errors. The spread of residuals also varies with
predicted strength. For the 14 test specimens with measured strength above
60 MPa, RMSE is 11.13 MPa and average residual is +3.67 MPa, indicating
underprediction on average.

CAUTIONS
- The older-age and high-strength results are based on only 14 test specimens
  in each subgroup, so they should be interpreted carefully.
- The plots describe model performance on this test split; they do not show
  that the model is accurate enough to verify a required design strength.
